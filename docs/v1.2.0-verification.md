## What changed

ISO 3166-1 entry: `official_name`, `currency_codes`,
`calling_codes`, `tlds`. The registry is fully enriched.

## Known limitations

- Wrappers remain source-installable. Publication to PyPI, npm, and
  crates.io is v3.0.0 work.
- The ISO 4217 mirror is still outstanding in that repository.
- `languages` and `borders` remain null for every entry; v1.3.0 work.
- The ITU snapshot contains ~50 more codes than the strict country
  list. They are valid ITU assignments but not all map to an ISO
  3166-1 entry. Tightening the snapshot is v1.3.0 work.
- Antarctica (AQ) has no currency; its `currency_codes` is an empty
  list. BL, BQ, EH, MF, UM have no IANA ccTLD; their `tlds` are
  empty. Both are recorded explicitly, not left null.
