## Known limitations

Unchanged from v1.0.0:

- `currency_codes`, `calling_codes`, `tlds`, `languages`, `borders`
  are null for every entry; targeted for v1.2.0 and v1.3.0.
- The withdrawn set is the representative 15 entries from v1.0.0,
  not the full ISO 3166-3 record. Full completion is v1.1.0 work.
- The exceptionally-reserved and user-assigned entries (`UK`, `EU`,
  `XK`) do not have `official_name`; ISO does not publish one for
  these codes.

## Correction (2026-09-23)

The v1.0.1 release shipped `tools/enrich_official_name.py` and the
`check-official-name` CI job, but not the 238-entry data population
the roadmap called for. The coverage table above has been corrected
from 249/249 to 11/249.

The check remains in CI as an advisory gate. The enrichment is
v1.2.0 work.
