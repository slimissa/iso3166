# v1.12.3 verification: errata

This file corrects `docs/v1.12.3-verification.md`. That report and its
`docs/v1.12.3-verification.md.sha256` are unchanged: appending a note to the
report would make `sha256sum -c` fail, and the hash is the record that the
report is the one the release wrote. The correction lives here, next to it.

## What is wrong

The report says `Result: PASS: all 11 commands exited 0` and lists 11 gate
commands. About 38 ran: 8 artifact freshness commands and the 30 lines of the
gate.

The 11 are not the outer run's. Items 1 to 8 (the seven `--check` commands
and the `git diff --exit-code`) are the records of the **last nested
`release.sh`**, scenario 9 of `tests/test_release_force.sh`, which runs inside
the gate and died after its own freshness step. Items 9 to 11
(`bash tests/test_release_force.sh`, `bash tools/check_cross_language.sh US GB
JP`, `cargo clippy`) are the last three lines of the real gate.

**Missing from the report:** the 27 gate lines that ran before
`tests/test_release_force.sh`, among them the version, claims and mojibake
checks, the snapshot freshness checks, `validate.py`, the seven `enrich_field`
checks, the introduced and club checks, `check_release_diff.py`, and the
Python test suites. The outer run's own freshness commands are missing too:
the ones shown are the nested run's.

## What is not wrong

The gate itself. Its pass or fail comes from the exit status of the gate
subshell (`GATE_RC`), not from the record file, and the tag was created only
after the gate returned 0. The record is evidence about the gate, and the
evidence was damaged.

## Cause

`release.sh` wrote the record to the fixed path `/tmp/release-gate.rec` and
truncated it at the start of each run. The force test runs a complete
`release.sh` in throwaway repositories from inside the gate, and each of those
runs truncated the outer run's record.

## Fix

v1.12.4 gives every run its own `mktemp` directory and ignores an inherited
record path. `tests/test_release_force.sh` scenarios 10 and 11 fail on the
v1.12.3 script and pass on the v1.12.4 one. Reports written from v1.12.4 on
carry the full record.
